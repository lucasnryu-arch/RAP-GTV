# Data availability

The repository does not redistribute any real-site raster, point product, Sentinel-2 image, EGMS product, DEM, or locally assembled archive.

| Site | Source type | Availability statement |
|---|---|---|
| Xiongba | Three-component cumulative InSAR displacement | Obtain from the original dataset provider or authors according to its terms. The adapter records DOI `10.17632/pss9p5p2n5.1`. |
| Offida | EGMS L2a product and IFFI polygon | Obtain from the official EGMS and Italian Landslide Inventory services under their terms. |
| Mendatica | Research displacement archive | Obtain from the original provider or authors according to its terms. |
| Zhouqu–Xieliupo | SBAS-InSAR point product | Obtain from the original provider or authors according to its terms. |
| All sites | Copernicus DEM and Sentinel-2 | Obtain from official Copernicus services under the applicable product terms. |

The complete computational pipeline is supplied. Exact site-specific reproduction requires the corresponding source data and the same preprocessing contract.
